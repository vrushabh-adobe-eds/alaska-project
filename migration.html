Executive Summary

alaskaair.com is a JavaScript-rendered Adobe Experience Manager site. The XML sitemap lists 4,970 URLs, but these resolve to 710 unique English-default pages replicated across 7 locales (en + en-au, en-gb, en-nz, it-it, ja-jp, ko-kr). A naive crawler under-counts because content loads via JS and most pages are unreachable from the top navigation, so the authoritative inventory is derived from the sitemap. Every HTTP status was re-verified live. Applying the "200 and not redirecting off-domain" rule leaves 611 in-scope pages — dominated by the content-article template (68 pages).

Key corrections & notes
  • Locale de-duplication — 4,970 sitemap URLs → 710 unique EN pages. The 6 non-default locales (4,260 URLs) are structural duplicates, tracked as a localization dimension, not counted as distinct pages.
  • Legal section — 42 of 58 /content/legal/* URLs 308-redirect to the asset host resource.alaskaair.net (Contract-of-Carriage PDFs) → document migration, not pages.
  • Hawaiian Airlines merger — several /content/about-us/* paths 308-redirect to hawaiianairlines.com → out of scope (off-domain).
  • curl 406 (shown as 200*) — bot-protection blocks on the HEAD request; those pages render live in a browser and were successfully analyzed → treated as in-scope live.
  • Transactional routes (booking, account, reset) are dynamic app flows — inventoried but flagged for custom build.
