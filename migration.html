alaskaair.com is a JavaScript-rendered Adobe Experience Manager site. The XML sitemap lists 4,970 URLs, but these resolve to 710 unique English-default pages replicated across 7 locales (en + en-au, en-gb, en-nz, it-it, ja-jp, ko-kr). A naive crawler under-counts because content loads via JS and most pages are unreachable from the top navigation. The authoritative inventory is derived from the sitemap urlset, and every HTTP status in this report was re-verified live. Applying the "200 and not redirecting off-domain" rule leaves 611 in-scope pages — dominated by the content-article template (68 pages).

Key Corrections & Scoping Decisions

  • Locale de-duplication: 4,970 sitemap URLs → 710 unique EN pages. The 6 non-default locales (4,260 URLs) are structural duplicates and were set aside as a localization dimension, not counted as distinct pages.
  • Legal section: 42 of 58 /content/legal/* URLs redirect (308) to the asset host resource.alaskaair.net — these are Contract-of-Carriage PDFs, handled as document migration, not pages.
  • Hawaiian Airlines merger: several /content/about-us/* paths 308-redirect to hawaiianairlines.com → out of scope (off-domain).
  • curl 406 responses (shown as 200*) are bot-protection blocks on the HEAD request; those pages render live in a real browser and were successfully analyzed — treated as in-scope live.
  • Transactional routes (booking, betaaccount, account, reset) are dynamic app flows — inventoried but flagged for custom build, not content migration.
